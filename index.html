Divergent Ankle Mechanisms Underlie Hindfoot Reversal in Therian Mammals

Duyi (Tina) Kuang & Stephanie E. Pierce · Harvard University

Do marsupials and placentals achieve hindfoot reversal through similar or different joint-level mechanisms?

X-ray reconstructions of an Eastern gray squirrel (a placental) and a Virginia opossum (a marsupial) during hindfoot reversal.
